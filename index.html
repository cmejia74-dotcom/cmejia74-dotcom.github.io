About the engineer

I'm an undergraduate student at Stanford, intending to major in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Aeronautics & Astronautics. I care about problems where clean geometry meets real physics — energy systems, aerodynamics, and anything where a small part has to do a specific job well.

I like taking a design all the way from CAD to a part that actually runs — modeling in Fusion 360, printing it, and testing whether it does what the math said it would. This page grows as I finish projects.
