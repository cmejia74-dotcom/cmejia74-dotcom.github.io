01

Automated Task Management System

A desk device that turns something you say out loud into a task on its screen. An ESP32 streams 16 kHz audio from an I2S microphone over Wi-Fi to a FastAPI server, which transcribes it and hands the text to an LLM that pulls out what the task actually is and sends it back to the display. I modeled the hinged enclosure in Fusion 360 so the screen mounts cleanly and the breadboard and wiring stay hidden.

  • ESP32
  • Fusion 360
  • Python
  • LLM API

BUILT · 2026 DWG CM-003
02

Small-Scale Wind Turbine

A CEE 34N team build. Our 3D-printed three-blade airfoil rotor wouldn't self-start, so we rebuilt it as a high-solidity drag rotor with wide paper sails. A hand-wound generator running six magnets on a printed shaft lights an LED, while the same shaft lifts a weight on a string. I designed and printed the blades.

About the engineer

I'm an undergraduate student at Stanford, majoring in Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Aeronautics & Astronautics. I care about problems where clean geometry meets real physics — energy systems, aerodynamics, and anything where a small part has to do a specific job well.

I like taking a design all the way from CAD to a part that actually runs — modeling it in Fusion 360, printing it, wiring up whatever electronics it needs, and writing the code to make the whole thing work. This page grows as I finish projects.
