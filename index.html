A sealed box for a solar car’s motor controller

Stanford Solar Car Project · Dynamics team · Fusion 360, ASA · 2026

The motor controller on Sunstruck, our car for the 2027 World Solar Challenge, needs to stay dry and stay put. I designed a water-resistant enclosure for it in Fusion 360, to be 3D printed in ASA and bolted to the carbon fiber chassis with four M6 bolts into threaded inserts.

It mounts by the wide flange at its back, with an M6 bolt in each corner and a loop of closed-cell EPDM foam sealing the flange against the chassis. The top is slanted so water runs off instead of sitting on it, and the controller’s connectors face down through openings projected from its own model and offset 0.5 mm for clearance.

An automated task manager you talk to

Independent project · ESP32, Fusion 360, Python · 2026

A desk device that turns something you say out loud into a task on its screen. An ESP32 streams 16 kHz audio from an I2S microphone over Wi-Fi to a FastAPI server, which transcribes it with Whisper and hands the text to a language model that pulls out what the task actually is. The result comes back to a 2.8-inch touchscreen.

I wired the electronics, wrote the firmware and the server, and modeled a hinged enclosure in Fusion 360 so the screen mounts cleanly and the breadboard and wiring stay hidden.

A wind turbine that had to lift and light

CEE 34N team project · 3D printing, generator design · 2026

Built with a team of three for CEE 34N, Wind Energy Explained. The brief was to lift a cup of coins on a string and light an LED with the same rotor. Our first rotor, three thin airfoil blades that I modeled and printed, would not start turning on its own, so we rebuilt it as a drag rotor with wide paper-bag sails that starts in gentle air.

A hand-wound generator with six magnets on a printed PLA shaft lights the LED, while the same shaft winds up the string. I designed and printed the blades and helped with assembly.
